About us

Bistro Verde Teste opened in 2015 as a small family kitchen. Ten years later we are still a small kitchen, with a bigger table.

A neighbourhood bistro where the menu follows the market, the bread comes out of our oven every morning and the wine comes from people we know.

Our suppliers

Our wines

From small producers we visit every year, many of them organic.
